Before we begin

A few good
questions.

Where does a branding project start?+

With a conversation about your audience, your goals and what makes your business different. We agree on the brief and scope before exploring visual directions.

Can you work with our existing identity?+

Yes. We can build on a strong foundation, refine an identity that needs attention, or explore a new direction. The brief decides which approach fits.

What will we receive?+

Deliverables are agreed in the proposal. They can include logo files, colour and typography guidance, templates, artwork and a practical brand guide.

How do we get a quote?+

Tell us what you need, where it will be used and your ideal timeline. We will discuss the scope and prepare a proposal.
