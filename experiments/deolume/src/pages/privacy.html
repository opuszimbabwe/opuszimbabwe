Privacy
& cookies.

Deolume is the global studio direction of Opus Zimbabwe. This separate design preview uses essential storage only.

What this preview stores

An essential cookie named deolume_consent remembers your cookie choice for up to 180 days. A session storage setting named deolume_motion remembers whether you paused decorative motion while this tab is open.

No analytics, advertising cookies, social tracking pixels, third-party fonts or embedded videos are installed. Media and fonts are served from this site. The hosting provider may process basic request information to deliver and secure the website.

Project enquiries

The enquiry form prepares a message in your email or WhatsApp app. It does not automatically submit to a server. You can review the message before sending. When you send it, the Opus Zimbabwe team receives the information you included and uses it to discuss your enquiry.

Email and WhatsApp are external services with their own privacy terms. Please include only information needed to begin the conversation.

Your choices

You can change your cookie choice with “Cookie settings” in the footer, pause motion using the motion control, or clear storage in your browser. Your operating system’s reduced-motion setting is also respected.
