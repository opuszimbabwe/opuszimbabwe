Cookie and browser-storage notice

Updated 18 November 2026

This notice describes the cookie choice shared between opuszim.co.zw and payments.opuszim.co.zw, and the storage used for chat, checkout and security.

One shared cookie choice

Selecting “Use essential only” or saving the essential-only preference stores a preference for 180 days. You can reopen Cookie preferences on either website. The shared preference is not a login, payment authorisation or customer identifier. It contains no name, phone number, card details, PIN, OTP or payment access token.

Analytics and advertising

We have not enabled optional advertising or analytics cookies in this preference panel. Cloudflare Web Analytics currently supplies a cookieless JavaScript beacon for usage and performance measurements. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not use cookies or localStorage for analytics. Infrastructure also processes requests and security information to deliver and protect the service.

The essential-only cookie setting is not an opt-out switch for Cloudflare’s cookieless measurements or operational security logs. We explain this distinction rather than presenting an inactive analytics-cookie switch. If optional tracking cookies are introduced, this notice and the available choices must be updated before enabling them.

Your controls

You can reopen Cookie preferences at any time, and clear cookies or browser storage in your browser. Clearing a conversation cookie or payment session data can remove this browser’s access to existing information. Blocking essential storage may prevent recovery, chat or protected access from working. Browser restrictions can prevent the shared preference from being saved.

Payment providers and external links

Hosted payment approval and external services have their own storage and policies. Opus’s cookie choice does not control Pesepay, Paynow, WhatsApp or a card issuer’s website. Enter a wallet PIN or card details only in the appropriate provider’s trusted flow.
