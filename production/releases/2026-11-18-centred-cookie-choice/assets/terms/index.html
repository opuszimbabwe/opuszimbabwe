Domain services are subject to availability, eligibility checks and the applicable registrar and registry agreements. Opus assists customers through registration providers; using our website does not mean Opus is an ICANN-accredited registrar or the registry for a particular extension.

Supply the actual domain owner’s accurate legal and contact details. Some registries require documentary evidence, email verification or publication of registration data. Privacy redaction depends on the registry and cannot be promised for every extension. You must have authority to register the name and must not infringe another party’s rights.

For supported automated registrations, verified payment authorises us to submit the approved registration to the supplier. Registration is complete only when supplier confirmation has been obtained. Availability can change before completion; price changes, ineligible details or uncertain supplier responses may require review. Local Zimbabwean domains currently follow an assisted enquiry and confirmation process rather than an automatic international-domain checkout.

Successful domain registrations and renewals are normally irreversible supplier transactions. Where a purchase has not completed, we will investigate the order and discuss fulfilment or refund of the unspent amount, subject to lawful, disclosed and genuinely incurred charges. Do not assume an uncertain order has failed and place duplicates.

Keep contact details current and renew by the stated deadline. Auto-renewal is not included unless explicitly enabled and agreed. Expiry, redemption, release and transfer rules are set by the relevant supplier or registry. We will explain access and transfer arrangements for domains managed on your behalf.

9. Maintenance, monitoring and reports

Monthly care is supplied per project at the selected plan’s published or agreed scope. The US$10 starting plan covers monitoring, incident and maintenance records and a concise report; it does not include unlimited repairs, redesign, hosting or development. Higher plans include only their stated content-update or maintenance allowances.

Included time resets monthly and does not roll over unless agreed otherwise. Standard support hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00–17:00 Zimbabwe time, excluding public holidays. Priority support means queue priority during those hours, not a guaranteed repair time.

Monitoring is sampled and can have coverage gaps. Reports distinguish recorded checks, failures, resolution evidence and unverified areas. A passed check does not prove uninterrupted availability, complete security or working backups. We do not remove genuine failures merely to produce an all-green report.

Payment-linked reports follow the agreed customer arrangement. A progress report can cover the period observed so far; a final report follows month-end when eligible. Email-provider acceptance is not proof of customer delivery.
