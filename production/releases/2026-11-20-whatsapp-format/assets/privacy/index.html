1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes the handling of personal information by Opus Zimbabwe (“Opus”, “we”, “us” or “our”) when you visit our website, contact us, use our checkout or receive our services. It applies alongside any project-specific confidentiality or data-processing agreement.

For our own enquiries, billing and support, we determine the relevant purposes of processing. Where we operate a customer’s website, school system, chatbot or other application on that customer’s instructions, our role and responsibilities depend on the project agreement. The customer’s own privacy notice may also apply.

Privacy contact: support@opuszim.co.zw. Please use the subject “Privacy request” and avoid sending sensitive identity documents until we have agreed a secure verification method.

2. Information we collect

The information involved depends on what you choose to do. We seek information relevant to the requested service, rather than requiring every visitor to provide the same details.

  • Enquiry and contact details: your name, organisation, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, service interests and the brief or files you provide.
  • Project information: content, approved materials, account identifiers, authorised instructions, access arrangements, correspondence and delivery records.
  • Payment and order records: payer details, invoice or order reference, amount, currency, method, provider reference, transaction status and receipt-delivery contact.
  • Domain-owner details: the legal registrant’s name, organisation, address, email, telephone and registry-specific eligibility information where required.
  • Support and messaging information: message content, supplied profile name, sender number, timestamps, delivery status, attachments and a contact photo where an authorised person provides one.
  • Technical and operational data: network and request information made available to our infrastructure, security events, website-check results, incident records and relevant publication or maintenance evidence.
  • Website feedback: the rating, optional comment and page path you submit. A daily pseudonymous request value is used for basic submission limits.
  • Website conversations: an optional name, message content, timestamps and attachments you choose to send, including voice recordings and camera photos.
  • Finance requests and agreements you send to Opus: your First Name as it appears on the birth certificate, surname, email, phone, address, optional organisation, requested amount, currency and term; confirmed amounts, repayment dates and schedule; signatures, signature dates, related correspondence and payment records where an arrangement proceeds.

3. How information reaches us

Most information comes directly from you or an authorised representative. It can also come from a payment or registration provider, a connected support platform, a client’s authorised project systems or public information needed to verify a service request.

Our contact form currently prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. The form explains that WhatsApp opens with the details filled in and that you choose whether to send. Once sent, the message passes through WhatsApp and can be received by our business messaging integration.

The official WhatsApp API does not supply ordinary customers’ profile photos to this inbox. A photo shown there may be a private image uploaded by authorised staff; otherwise we display initials. We do not claim to extract a photo from a personal WhatsApp account.

The website feedback form sends your chosen rating and comment to our business records. Website chat uses its own browser conversation session. Camera and microphone access is requested only when you select the relevant control; captured media is uploaded when you choose to send it.

4. Why we use information

We use information for the service or business purpose associated with the interaction, including:

  • Responding to enquiries, preparing quotations and agreeing a project scope.
  • Delivering, administering and supporting websites, software, hosting, email, design and integrations.
  • Verifying payments, reconciling orders, investigating failures or reversals and producing receipts.
  • Submitting authorised domain requests and meeting registrar or registry requirements.
  • Monitoring enrolled projects, recording incidents and producing maintenance reports.
  • Protecting accounts and systems, preventing fraud and investigating misuse.
  • Keeping appropriate accounting, contractual and compliance records, and resolving disputes.
  • Reviewing finance requests you send us, discussing and documenting agreed terms, administering an accepted arrangement, crediting payments, responding to questions and resolving a related dispute.

5. Lawful and fair processing

Depending on the interaction and applicable law, processing may be necessary to take steps you request before a contract, perform an agreement, comply with a legal obligation, protect a legitimate business or security interest, or act on your consent. Where consent is required, we will seek it for the relevant purpose.

We do not rely on a single blanket consent for every activity. You may withdraw consent for an optional activity, but withdrawal does not invalidate processing already lawfully performed or remove records we must keep for another lawful reason. We do not sell customer personal information.

6. Payments and receipts

Pesepay, Paynow and the selected financial provider process payment authorisation for the methods available in Opus Pay. Card details are entered on the payment provider’s checkout. We retain the order, provider reference, amount, currency, status and verification details needed to administer your payment. Do not give our staff or assistant your mobile-money PIN, banking password or card security code. Approval codes entered in a designated wallet payment flow are used for that request; they should not be sent in chat.

Receipt emails are sent to the address supplied or otherwise authorised for the order. Private receipt or document links may contain access tokens. Treat them as confidential: anyone you intentionally share an active link with may be able to view the corresponding document.

A payment dispute may require us to share relevant transaction details with the payment provider, your authorised representative or an appropriate authority. We do not publish personal payment records on the public website.

7. Finance requests and signed documents

Opus Loaners is intended for selected known and trusted contacts, not the general public. The /loaners page lets those contacts download a blank agreement and try a simple-interest illustration. Keeping this page out of search results does not make its link or downloadable form private. Illustration values remain in the page’s browser state and are not submitted to a finance endpoint. Editing or signing a downloaded PDF does not automatically upload it to Opus. A completed form reaches us only when you send or otherwise provide it through an agreed channel.

When Opus receives a completed form, we use the relevant identity and contact details, requested amount and term, agreed repayment information, signatures and correspondence to review your request, communicate with you, prepare and administer an accepted arrangement, record payments, meet applicable obligations and handle disputes. Depending on the activity, the applicable basis may be steps you request before an agreement, performance of an agreement, a legal obligation, an appropriate legitimate interest or specific consent where required. Providing a form is not blanket consent for unrelated advertising.

We request a first name as it appears on the birth certificate to help record the correct name. The form does not require uploading a birth certificate, identity-document copy, bank password or payment PIN. The brand or organisation field is optional. Ask us before sending any additional sensitive evidence, and use the agreed secure channel.

Completed agreements and signature information are private business records. Access should be limited to authorised people handling the request or arrangement. Relevant information may be shared with an authorised financial or payment provider when needed, professional advisers under appropriate duties, or a competent authority where lawfully required. No credit-bureau reporting, external credit scoring or automated approval decision is activated by the current document-download page. Any future feature of that kind requires an appropriate notice and lawful basis before it is enabled.

A signature image or typed name may identify you and record acceptance; a certificate signature can also carry certificate or verification information supplied by your signing application. We do not use the current PDF feature for biometric identification. Keep completed copies and any private links secure. You can contact support@opuszim.co.zw to ask about access, correction, the purpose of a requested field, or deletion where permitted.

8. Domains, hosting and customer systems

An authorised domain registration requires sharing the relevant registrant and eligibility details with the selected registrar or supplier and registry. Their registration terms and privacy practices apply to their processing. Some registries publish registration data; redaction is available only where supported.

For local .co.zw, .org.zw and .ac.zw applications, we collect the applicant’s contact details, organisation, address, core business, preferred name and optional name servers. A signed or stamped letter on organisational letterhead is required for .org.zw and .ac.zw. Files are private to authorised staff and used for eligibility and registration enquiries, including submission to the relevant registrar or registry as authorised. Applications and supporting letters are retained for up to 12 months unless a legal requirement requires longer retention.

Hosting and business email can involve providers that store or transmit website content, messages and account data. The infrastructure and access required depend on the purchased service. We do not claim that all hosting includes backups, verified recovery or unlimited storage.

Where a project concerns children, school records, health information or other sensitive data, the customer must establish the appropriate authority and instructions. Additional safeguards and contractual arrangements may be necessary before that information is processed. We do not request children’s personal details for routine marketing enquiries.

9. WhatsApp and AI-assisted features

Messages sent to our API-connected business number can be stored in a protected business inbox so authorised staff can read and respond. This business-side processing is separate from your personal phone’s WhatsApp history. Delivery events help us distinguish queued, sent, delivered, read, failed and uncertain messages.

Opus provides AI-assisted enquiries and business automation alongside human support. AI-assisted replies are generated automatically and are distinct from replies personally written by a human. For conversations handled by an assistant, relevant messages, supplied images or a brief are processed by the AI provider supporting that feature to help answer or organise the request. AI-assisted WhatsApp replies and human replies use the business messaging integration and remain subject to Meta’s rules and account availability. You can request human assistance or ask us to stop optional messaging.

Do not send payment PINs, passwords, unnecessary identity documents or confidential third-party information to an assistant. You may ask for human help. We use system checks, rather than an AI’s statement alone, to verify payments and authorise protected actions. AI output can be wrong and should not be treated as specialist legal, medical or financial advice.

Website chat is a separate conversation with our team. Messages and private attachments are stored in our systems for authorised staff to read and answer. Choosing the direct WhatsApp link opens that service; attachments from a website conversation are not automatically transferred into WhatsApp.

Staff can opt in to browser push alerts. These alerts are routed through the device’s notification provider and contain a generic new-message notice, without customer message text. Browser and operating-system settings affect whether an alert is shown.

10. Service providers and other disclosures

We share relevant information only as needed for the purpose concerned. Categories of recipients include infrastructure and storage providers, payment processors, domain registrars and registries, email-delivery providers, messaging platforms, and approved project suppliers. Examples in our current operations include Cloudflare, Pesepay, Paynow, Meta’s WhatsApp platform and Resend; the provider used for a particular domain or hosting service can vary.

AI providers process the relevant information used by our AI-assisted features. This does not mean every support conversation is sent to an AI provider. Provider terms govern their processing; we do not promise a confidentiality, training or retention arrangement that their applicable terms do not support.

We may also disclose information where you authorise it, to professional advisers subject to appropriate duties, to enforce or defend a lawful claim, or where required by a court or competent authority. We do not give other customers access to your private inbox or billing records.

Our support mailbox currently uses Zoho Mail. If you email a completed agreement, Zoho processes the email and attachments under its service arrangements. An automated email assistant is not currently connected to that mailbox.

11. International processing

Some service providers and their systems operate outside Zimbabwe. Information may therefore be processed or stored in another country, subject to the supplier’s arrangements and applicable law. We assess the relevant purpose and safeguards rather than promising that all information remains in Zimbabwe.

Where additional contractual terms, consent or another lawful transfer mechanism is required, it should be established before the relevant transfer. Contact us if your organisation has specific location or data-processing requirements so we can assess them before agreeing the service.

12. Cookies, browser storage and links

Our site uses essential cookies and browser storage for security, authenticated administrative access, website conversations, checkout and reliable operation. A shared preference cookie records the essential-only choice across opuszim.co.zw and payments.opuszim.co.zw for 180 days. It contains a preference value, not payment details or login credentials. Closing the notice alone dismisses it without saving that choice; cookie preferences can be reopened from the footer. The site also uses browser storage and content-version signals to refresh website content, while private checkout or document access can depend on a temporary capability token.

No optional advertising or tracking cookies are enabled by the current preference control. Cloudflare provides cookieless website performance and usage measurements; saving the essential-only preference does not switch those measurements off. Any future non-essential technology requiring consent will need a separate appropriate choice before activation. You can manage browser storage through your browser, although disabling essential functions can prevent sign-in, conversation access or checkout from working. Our Cookie Notice gives further details.

Links to WhatsApp, payment services and other external websites take you to services with their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control every aspect of their processing.

The website conversation cookie identifies the conversation in your browser. It is Secure, HttpOnly and restricted to this website. Clearing it removes that browser’s access to the conversation. Staff notification subscriptions are optional and can be removed through the workspace or the browser’s notification settings.

13. How long we keep information

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose, an active engagement, a legal requirement, a legitimate security investigation or a dispute. Accounting and contractual records may need to be kept longer than routine support information. We do not promise immediate deletion from all records and backups on request.

For WhatsApp messaging, the configured routine cleanup removes known-terminal message records older than 90 days and empty old conversations. Queued, sending or uncertain work can remain until reviewed. Minimal hashed webhook delivery evidence has a 30-day cleanup threshold, applied when subsequent deliveries are processed.

Uploaded contact photos are removed or replaced through the protected inbox controls. Customer attachments remain subject to Meta’s availability and retention rules when retrieved through the API. Project reports, receipts, incident histories and backups can have different retention needs. Please contact us for the arrangements relevant to your engagement.

Website conversation sessions expire after 90 days; routine maintenance removes expired conversations and associated private files. Website feedback is normally retained for up to 12 months. Notification endpoints remain while enabled and are removed after expiry or a staff removal request.

For finance requests received by Opus, we retain identifying details and completed agreements only while reasonably needed to review the request, administer an active arrangement, meet applicable accounting or legal recordkeeping requirements, or resolve a dispute. We do not keep a declined or withdrawn request in active use without a continuing lawful purpose. Contact support@opuszim.co.zw about the retention period relevant to your request and any access, correction or deletion request; legal obligations can limit deletion. The website does not apply an automatic deletion timer to documents held in staff email or other authorised business records.

14. Security and incident handling

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the service, including authenticated administrative access, restricted private records, encrypted secret configuration and checks on incoming provider requests. No security measure makes every system risk-free.

You should protect your own devices, accounts, approval channels and private order links. Tell us promptly if you suspect an unauthorised action or disclosure. We will investigate and make any notifications required by applicable law and our contractual obligations. A successful uptime check is not a complete security assessment.

15. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable law and necessary identity checks, you may request access to information about you, correction of inaccurate information, deletion or restriction where appropriate, or object to processing where the law allows. You may also withdraw consent for an activity based on consent and raise a concern about how information has been handled.

We will consider requests fairly and explain any lawful reason that prevents full compliance, such as a required financial record or an unresolved dispute. We may need to verify identity or your authority to represent another person, using a proportionate and secure process.

If the information belongs to a customer organisation’s application that we process only on its instructions, we may refer the request to that organisation and assist it as agreed. Contact support@opuszim.co.zw to begin. You may also raise a concern with the competent Zimbabwean data-protection authority; POTRAZ is the designated Data Protection Authority.

16. Policy updates and contact

We update this policy when our services, processing or legal obligations materially change. The date at the top identifies the current version. Where a change requires notice or consent, we will take the appropriate steps rather than treating a website edit as permission for an unrelated use.
